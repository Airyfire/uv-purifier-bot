# 💡 Inspiration & Prior Art

## Existing Designs Reviewed

### 1. Hospital UVGI Systems
- UV Germicidal Irradiation integrated into HVAC or built into walls/ceilings
- **Most effective** method for large-scale air sterilization
- ❌ Far too costly for a personal/student project
- Serves as the gold standard benchmark for UV air sterilization

### 2. UV-C Disinfection Robots (Commercial)
- Open UV-C emitters mounted on wheeled platforms
- Used in hospitals to sanitize rooms (e.g., Xenex, UVD Robots)
- ❌ Expose UV-C openly — require room to be vacated entirely
- ✅ **Inspired the mobile robot form factor** for this project

### 3. Autonomous Air Cleaning Bots
- Use vacuums + HEPA filters on mobile platforms
- ✅ Proven concept for mobile air cleaning
- ❌ No UV sterilization — bacteria and viruses are captured but not reliably neutralized

## Our Innovation

**Combine the best of all three:**
- Mobile platform (from autonomous cleaning bots)
- UV sterilization (from UV-C disinfection bots) — but **safely enclosed**
- HEPA filtration (from air cleaning bots)
- **Novel: target stagnant dead zones** that stationary purifiers miss
